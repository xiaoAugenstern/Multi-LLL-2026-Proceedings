Generative AI is reshaping educational feedback, offering new opportunities to provide personalised, timely, and scalable support for teaching and learning. However, its potential extends beyond automating feedback generation to enhancing feedback quality and effectiveness through meaningful human--AI collaboration. Drawing on a systematic review and empirical studies, this talk examines how AI can support educational feedback practices, from generating personalised feedback to analysing feedback quality and facilitating human--AI collaborative feedback processes. Particular attention is given to how AI can complement educators' pedagogical expertise, support informed decision-making, and enable the provision of actionable and contextually relevant feedback. The talk concludes by highlighting key opportunities and challenges in developing human--AI collaborative approaches that enhance feedback practices and improve student learning experiences.
