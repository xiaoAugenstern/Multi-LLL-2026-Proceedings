Guanliang Chen is a Professor at Beijing Normal University. His research lies at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Learning Analytics, and Education, with a particular focus on developing responsible AI technologies to support large-scale, personalised learning, assessment, and feedback. He has published over 100 peer-reviewed journal and conference papers in leading international venues across AI, educational technology, and learning sciences, accumulating more than 10,000 citations on Google Scholar with an h-index of 40.
