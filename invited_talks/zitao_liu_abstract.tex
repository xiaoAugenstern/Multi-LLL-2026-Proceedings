In this talk I will present our work on multimodal language technologies for personalized learning, connecting foundational research with a deployed product. I will show how we model a learner's knowledge state from text, images, handwriting, and speech using interpretable knowledge-tracing models, together with a standardized evaluation platform and a large open dataset XES3G5M. I will then describe our multimodal educational resources and models for OCR, speech, and handwritten-formula recognition, and how we improve mathematical reasoning in small models. Finally, I will introduce Xiao Jing Long, a student-facing AI study companion on learning devices: it reads photos of homework and handwritten answers, identifies each day's most important weak point, and guides a personalized 15--20-minute learning session.
