Figurative language such as metaphor and synaesthesia poses great challenges for natural language understanding and second language acquisition, due to its non-literal and cognition-driven nature. This talk presents our research on figurative-language understanding from textual to multimodal and Chinese cross-lingual perspectives. We revisit textual metaphor detection with traditional neural and BERT-based models, and introduce our Chain-of-Thought empowered multimodal metaphor detection framework leveraging both image and text signals. We further explore Chinese synaesthesia: we build a human-annotated dataset, and address synaesthesia detection via joint extraction and tree-structured generation models. Our findings provide insights for cognition-aware NLP and figurative language processing across modalities and languages.
